## Welcome to the ADHRI-NLP Lab!

Our research lab focuses on diverse verticals within Natural Language Processing (NLP). We primarily explore the following areas:

* **Mechanistic Interpretability & Subspace Geometry**
* **Reasoning through Discrete and Latent Spaces in Large Language Models (LLMs)**
* **Trustworthy AI & Safety**
* **Multilingual Large Language Models (MLLMs) and Small Language Models (SLMs)**
* **Multimodal Fusion & Vision-Language Models**
* **Sustainability and Climate NLP**

At the ADHRI-NLP Lab, our team consists of PhD scholars, M.Tech students, and enthusiastic B.Tech students who collaborate to study these emerging research directions. Through interdisciplinary collaboration and hands-on experimentation, we aim to advance the understanding and development of robust, interpretable, and socially responsible NLP systems.

Through our work, we hope to contribute to cutting-edge research, practical applications, and responsible innovation in artificial intelligence and natural language processing.

Thank you for visiting our lab and learning more about our research.
